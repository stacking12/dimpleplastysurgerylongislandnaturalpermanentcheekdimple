About the Procedure

What Is Dimpleplasty Surgery?

Dimpleplasty Surgery is a quick cosmetic procedure that creates dimples in the cheeks by forming a small, subtle attachment between the skin and the underlying muscle. When you smile, the skin gently draws inward, creating a natural-looking dimple.

Common Questions

Dimpleplasty Surgery FAQs

1. What is Dimpleplasty Surgery?

Dimpleplasty Surgery is a minimally invasive cosmetic procedure that creates dimples in the cheeks. A small attachment is formed between the skin and the underlying facial muscle so that a dimple appears when you smile.

2. Is Dimpleplasty Surgery painful?

The procedure is typically performed with local anesthesia, so the area is numbed. Most patients report mild soreness, swelling, or tightness afterward, which is usually manageable. Your surgeon will explain your aftercare in detail.

3. Are the results permanent?

Dimpleplasty Surgery is designed to create long-lasting dimples. Individual results can vary with healing, facial structure, and aging, so a consultation is the best way to set realistic expectations.

4. How long is the recovery?

Many patients return to light daily activities within a few days. Swelling and a small amount of bruising can take a little longer to settle, and dimples may look more pronounced at first. Your care team will give you personalized recovery instructions.

5. Who is a good candidate for cheek dimple surgery?

Good candidates are healthy adults who want natural-looking dimples and have realistic expectations. The best way to find out if you qualify is to book a consultation, where we assess your facial anatomy and discuss your goals.
